Jade Trade is a decentralized, privacy-first crypto analytics terminal and global community interface designed for zero-data collection and local sessions. Built around an interactive TradingView technical chart and real-time Binance liquidity feeds for the top 50 cryptocurrency assets, the platform combines robust charting capabilities with a customizable Algorithmic Signal Engine.

Key features and components of the interface include:

Live Analyzer & Interactive Charting: Users can instantly switch between top crypto assets via quickpick menus or a trending market ribbon, while utilizing an independent calculation timeframe selector (ranging from 1-minute to daily intervals) and dedicated update controls.

Configurable Signal Engine: Traders can customize and test multiple technical parameters—including RSI oscillator thresholds, Moving Average types (SMA/EMA) and lengths, MACD trend settings, and volume spike multipliers—to evaluate market momentum instantly.

Algorithmic Trade Bias Panel: Positioned side-by-side with the chart for seamless visibility, the engine computes a live composite score and visual confidence badge, delivering a clear Long, Short, or Neutral market bias accompanied by an automated analysis rationale.

Automated Daily News Feed: Features a curated collection of over 50 automated market updates, regulatory flashes, and blockchain breakthroughs equipped with full pagination and direct verified article links.

Global Community Chat: An anonymous, peer-to-peer communication channel with automated nickname generation and rate-limiting to facilitate open trader discussions without requiring user registration.

Support & Donation Hub: Includes integrated options for community members to support ongoing development securely via GCash QR codes or PayPal links.

Overall, Jade Trade serves as a streamlined, client-side toolkit for traders seeking real-time technical analysis, custom algorithmic scoring, and community engagement in a secure environment.
